Questions before you book

Your quote depends on the items, journey, number of movers and access at both addresses. Send the postcodes, a list or photos, and your preferred date. We’ll explain whether an hourly rate or a fixed quote suits the job, and confirm what’s included before you book.

You don’t need to guess. Tell us what you’re moving, share photos of larger items, and mention stairs or awkward access. We’ll recommend a suitable van and the help needed. A few boxes, a heavy sofa and a full flat move can all need different arrangements.

We plan the loading around your furniture and fragile items, using protective blankets and securing the load for the journey. Tell us about valuable or delicate belongings in advance. Packing requirements and any applicable insurance cover should be confirmed before you book.

The quote sets out the van, number of movers and agreed loading, transport and unloading. Tell us if you need packing, furniture dismantling or help carrying items upstairs. We’ll confirm these services and any additional costs in writing, so you know what you’re booking.

Please mention the floor, whether there’s a lift, and how close the van can park at each address. Let us know about loading bays, permits or narrow doorways too. We’ll factor these into the plan and explain any agreed charges before booking.

Call or WhatsApp us with the date, postcodes and what needs moving. We’ll check the van and team’s availability and tell you which slots are possible. For a fixed moving day, contact us early. If your plans change, get in touch to discuss rescheduling and the agreed booking terms.

A website tailored to your moving business

The structure is ready. Your services, prices, photos and contact details turn it into a website for your own business.

Your look and identity

Your logo and colours replace the demo branding. We use your photographs to show your vans, team and work. If suitable photos aren’t available, realistic generated images can be discussed separately.

Your services and terms

We keep the services you actually offer and explain how customers can book them. Prices, service areas, opening hours and quote conditions reflect the way you work.

Clear, connected content

Each section answers a customer question, from choosing the right service to understanding the quote and making an enquiry. You don’t have to write every page from scratch.

Ready for launch

Your contact links and enquiry forms are connected as part of the agreed launch setup. Privacy and cookie pages need your business details and review before launch.

A website that can grow with your business

Start with a focused website for your current services. Once it’s adapted and launched, you can use it for advertising and introduce new customers to your company.

Further work can be added when there’s a clear business need:

  • Extra sections. Explain specialist services or what makes your team different.
  • Quote tools. Offer estimates based on your own pricing rules.
  • Service pages. Give each type of move more space and detail.
  • Helpful articles. Answer customers’ questions about preparing for a move.
  • Search optimisation. Develop pages around relevant local searches.
  • New features. Support the next stage of your business.

The scope and price of any extra work are agreed before it starts. A fully bespoke website is also available as a separate project.
